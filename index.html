About This Archive

This website documents the history of enterprise storage arrays, from legacy systems like EMC Symmetrix DMX and IBM XIV to modern all-flash platforms like Everpure FlashArray and Dell PowerMax.

📢 Rebrand Updates:
• Feb 2026: Pure Storage → Everpure
• Post-2016: EMC → Dell EMC (Symmetrix→PowerMax, Isilon→PowerScale, VNX→Unity/PowerStore)

Each entry includes release year, specifications, and downloadable datasheets where available.
